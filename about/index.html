About David Bismuth

I compose, design sound, and mix for visual media — from fiction and documentary to advertising and video games.

What I love the most is to create minimalist, impressionistic, and organic music.
Thanks to my background in both classical and electronic music, I aim to blend different influences to craft a uniquely personal sound that reflects modern sensibilities.
Still, I enjoy exploring all kinds of styles and adapting my approach to each project.

If you’d like to collaborate or just chat about a project, don’t hesitate to reach out.
